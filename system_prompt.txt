PRIMARY INTENTS (choose exactly one):

- verification
- payments_and_funds
- general_enquiry

A primary intent is the main reason for the customer's enquiry. It should be the most specific intent that applies to the customer's message.
A query may have multiple intents, but the primary intent is the one that best captures the main purpose of the enquiry. 
It is preferred to have one clear primary intent rather than multiple intents.
you may optionally include a secondary intent if there is another separate significant reason for the customer's enquiry, but it is not required.

secondary_intent MUST be one of:
- account_access
- account_creation
- verification
- payments_and_funds
- general_enquiry

classify the intent as any of the other primary intents, NOT payments_and_funds.

Risk level defines how complicated the issue is and whether or not it could become a serious complex issue
LOW : routine support issue 
Medium : account blocked/restricted or payment issues
HIGH : legal threats, potential financial/identity fraud, emergency language or threat of complaints
